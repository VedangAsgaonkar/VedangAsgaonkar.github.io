Vedang Asgaonkar

MS Computer Science, Stanford University

I'm a first-year MSCS student at Stanford, specializing in AI. I work with Prof. Jure Leskovec on the Relational Transformer, a foundation model for in-context prediction on relational databases.

Before Stanford I spent two years as a quantitative researcher at Tower Research Capital in London and New York. I studied computer science at IIT Bombay, where I graduated first in my batch and received the President of India Gold Medal for my academic achievements. There I did research on feature acquisition, time-series retrieval and evolutionary game theory.
