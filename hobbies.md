Here is what I would like the world to believe I do in my free time—which may or may not exist. Open the box to find out 🐱📦.

## Music 🎧
I generally like soulful and lo-fi songs, typically with a good amount of guitar and piano. My playlists would only partially agree. They know that [I put my armor on to show you how strong I am](https://music.youtube.com/watch?v=cfhwSpSDA9c).

## TV shows and movies 🎦
I prefer light TV shows, generally sitcoms. My taste in movies is the opposite: strong story lines, some drama, and a twist.

## Sports ⚾
I enjoy working out, swimming and playing ping-pong. I generally prefer sports that are played solo or in small teams.
